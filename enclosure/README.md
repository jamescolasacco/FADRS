# Enclosure
There are different enclosures for each FADRS module. They are made to fit the provided PCBs properly. Once machined, the enclosure can be improved by adding the recommended materials as directed below.

## NOTE:
Instructions are very early in development and are likely to change.

## Print Settings (recommended)
- **Infill:** 20%
- **Supports:** Everywhere; Organic/Tree Style
- **Layer Height:** 0.2mm

## Parts (recommended)
- **PCB Screws:** M3 6mm Pan Head (x4)
- **Faceplate Screws:** M3 6mm Flat Head (x4)
- **Potentiometer Gasket:** Black Felt Sheet (cut to fit fader, glue to underside of faceplate)

## Assembly (work in progress)
- **Fix PCB:** Align pcb and fix into the enclosure base with M3 screws
- **Attach Faceplate:** Secure the faceplate over the PCB so the electronics align and fix with the faceplate screws
